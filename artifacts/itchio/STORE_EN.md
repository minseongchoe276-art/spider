# SHADOW PULSE

## Short description
Swing across the city with energy wires. Explore solo or chase your friends in a third-person action playground.

## Store description
Make your own route above the streets. Attach an energy wire to a building, build momentum, and release to carry yourself across the skyline.

**SHADOW PULSE** is a browser-based action game in development, combining two-handed wire swinging, wall traversal, aerial front flips, and close combat. No installation required.

### In this demo
- Hold either mouse button to maintain a wire from that hand. Release to coast on your momentum.
- Connect jumps, short aerial zips, wall climbing, and rooftop traversal.
- Start in a compact procedural city, or choose Hong Kong with streamed external 3D city data.
- Explore solo, share a free-roam room, or play 2–8-player cops-and-runners matches online.
- Switch to combat for jabs, crosses, hooks, blocking, and dodging. Runners cannot attack during cops-and-runners matches.

### Getting started
Click **Launch game**, wait for loading, then choose **혼자 플레이** (Solo). Click the game to control the camera. Move with WASD, aim at a tall building, and hold a mouse button to swing. Fullscreen is recommended. Press H for the controls guide and Esc for settings.

### Controls
WASD: move · Shift: sprint · Space: jump / leap off wires · Left/right mouse: wire · E: aerial zip · F+W: wall climb · T: aerial front flip · Q: switch wire/combat mode · H: help · M: map · Esc: menu.
In combat: left mouse jab, right mouse cross, E hook, G heavy cross, hold B to block, Ctrl+direction to dodge.

### Before you play
This is a work-in-progress demo released primarily for desktop keyboard and mouse. **The in-game interface is primarily Korean.** Use a current WebGL-capable desktop browser with hardware acceleration. Initial loading and streamed Hong Kong terrain can take time. Fixed minimum hardware specifications and frame-rate targets have not been established.

Online rooms and Hong Kong terrain require internet access and external services. Custom team/FFA matches are not included in this itch.io build. Mobile and gamepad input are in development; desktop is the primary supported environment for this release.

The cover and banner are AI-generated promotional illustrations. Gallery screenshots show actual gameplay.
